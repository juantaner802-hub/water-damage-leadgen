Why a Flooded Basement Is a Time-Sensitive Emergency

Davenport sits along the Mississippi River, making basement flooding one of the most common disasters homeowners face — especially during spring thaw and heavy storm seasons. Whether caused by a sump pump failure, heavy rainfall, groundwater seepage, or a burst pipe, standing water must be addressed immediately.

Water migrates fast. Within two hours it saturates drywall, flooring, and wood framing. Within 24 hours mold spores germinate. What could have been a $2,000 cleanup can become a $15,000+ rebuild if you wait.
