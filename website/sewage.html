Do Not Enter the Affected Area

Sewage water — also known as "black water" in the restoration industry — is classified as a Category 3 biohazard. It contains E. coli, hepatitis, and dozens of other dangerous pathogens. Direct contact can cause serious illness. Keep children and pets away from the area entirely until our certified technicians complete the cleanup and sanitation.

Our Sewage Cleanup Process

  • Full PPE deployment — our technicians are equipped for biohazard environments
  • Containment of the affected area to prevent cross-contamination
  • Extraction and removal of all sewage water and solid waste
  • Removal of all contaminated porous materials (carpet, drywall, insulation)
  • Hospital-grade disinfection and antimicrobial treatment of all surfaces
  • Industrial deodorization — ozone treatment and hydroxyl generators
  • Air quality testing after remediation
  • Full documentation for insurance claims

Common Causes of Sewage Backup in Davenport

  • Municipal sewer line overflows during heavy rain or flooding
  • Clogged main sewer line from roots, grease buildup, or debris
  • Failed or overwhelmed septic system
  • Blocked floor drain or toilet backup
  • Mississippi River flooding causing reverse flow in sewer lines

Insurance and Sewage Cleanup

Coverage for sewage backup varies by policy. Some standard homeowner's policies include a sewer backup rider, while others require a separate endorsement. We'll help you review your policy and document everything required to file a successful claim. In many cases, restoration is fully or substantially covered.
