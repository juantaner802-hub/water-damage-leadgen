What To Do Right Now

If a pipe has burst in your home, take these steps immediately while you wait for us to arrive:

  • Shut off your main water supply — find the shutoff valve and turn it off immediately
  • Turn off electricity in affected areas if water is near outlets or the panel
  • Do not use wet-dry vacuums — they're not built for this volume
  • Move valuables out of the water path if safe to do so
  • Call us immediately — the faster we arrive, the less damage occurs

Why Davenport Pipes Burst

Iowa winters are brutal. When temperatures drop below freezing — especially in unheated spaces like crawl spaces, garages, or exterior walls — water inside pipes freezes and expands, creating pressure that splits the pipe. When temperatures rise again, the break releases and water flows freely.

Common locations for burst pipes in Davenport homes include crawl spaces, attic spaces, exterior walls, pipes near garage doors, and vacation or rental properties where heat may have been turned down too low.

Does Insurance Cover Burst Pipes?

In most cases, yes. Sudden and accidental discharge from a burst pipe is typically covered under standard homeowner's insurance policies. We carry detailed documentation of all damage and work directly with your adjuster to ensure your claim is processed correctly and completely.
